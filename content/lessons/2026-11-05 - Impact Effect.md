# 2026-11-05 — Impact / effect (Lesson 25)

## Quick review（Lesson 24）

1. **play a pivotal role in** — 喺……入面發揮關鍵作用
2. **be of paramount importance** — 極為重要
3. **serve as a cornerstone of** — 作為……嘅基石
4. **lie at the heart of** — 處於……嘅核心
5. **be indispensable to** — 對……不可或缺

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. have far-reaching consequences for
- Weak → better: *affect many things* → **have far-reaching consequences for**
- 意思：對……造成深遠影響
- Frame: `have far-reaching consequences for + person / group / issue`
- Model: A major change to the transport network can **have far-reaching consequences for** neighbourhood businesses.
- Common mistake: ~~consequences to local shops~~ → *consequences for local shops*

### 2. have a knock-on effect on
- Weak → better: *cause another effect on* → **have a knock-on effect on**
- 意思：對……產生連鎖影響
- Frame: `have a knock-on effect on + noun`
- Model: Longer travel times can **have a knock-on effect on** workers' childcare arrangements.
- Common mistake: ~~a knock-on effect to housing~~ → *a knock-on effect on housing*

### 3. make a significant difference to
- Weak → better: *help a lot* → **make a significant difference to**
- 意思：對……帶來顯著改變
- Frame: `make a significant difference to + noun`
- Model: Step-free access can **make a significant difference to** passengers with mobility needs.
- Common mistake: ~~make a significant difference for access~~ → *make a significant difference to access*

### 4. exert a positive influence on
- Weak → better: *encourage* → **exert a positive influence on**
- 意思：對……發揮正面影響
- Frame: `exert a positive influence on + behaviour / choice / outcome`
- Model: Reliable train services can **exert a positive influence on** commuters' travel choices.
- Common mistake: ~~influence to travel choices~~ → *influence on travel choices*

### 5. take a toll on
- Weak → better: *harm / make life harder for* → **take a toll on**
- 意思：對……造成損害／帶來負面影響
- Frame: `take a toll on + person / health / daily life`
- Model: Frequent delays can **take a toll on** commuters' well-being.
- Common mistake: ~~take a toll to commuters~~ → *take a toll on commuters*

## Mini drill

**A.** 填空：A new rail line can ________ ________ ________ ________ neighbourhood businesses.
**B.** 選正確介詞：Delays can take a toll (**on** / to) commuters.
**C.** 改錯：Better bus connections have a knock-on effect to housing demand.

## Reading passage

Hong Kong's new rail links could **have far-reaching consequences for** the communities they connect. Shorter journeys may bring more customers to local shops, while rising housing demand could **have a knock-on effect on** rents. For older residents and passengers with mobility needs, lifts and step-free routes can **make a significant difference to** everyday travel. Frequent, reliable services can also **exert a positive influence on** people's choices by making public transport easier to use. By contrast, delays and overcrowding may **take a toll on** commuters' well-being.

**Passage tasks**
1. List the five taught collocations used in the passage.
2. In one sentence, name one possible effect of new rail links on local shops or housing.
3. Match: lifts and step-free routes → ? ; reliable services → ? ; delays and overcrowding → ?（選：positive influence on travel choices / difference to everyday travel / toll on commuters' well-being）

## ANSWER KEY

### Mini drill
- A: **have far-reaching consequences for**
- B: **on**
- C: Better bus connections have a knock-on effect **on** housing demand.

### Passage tasks
1. **have far-reaching consequences for**; **have a knock-on effect on**; **make a significant difference to**; **exert a positive influence on**; **take a toll on**
2. Shorter journeys may bring more customers to local shops / rising housing demand could affect rents.
3. Lifts and step-free routes → **difference to everyday travel**; reliable services → **positive influence on travel choices**; delays and overcrowding → **toll on commuters' well-being**
