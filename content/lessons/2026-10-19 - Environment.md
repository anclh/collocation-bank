## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. tackle climate change
- Weak → better: *deal with / try to stop climate change* → **tackle climate change**
- 意思：認真處理氣候變化問題
- Frame: `governments / countries / cities + tackle climate change`
- Model: Nations must work together to **tackle climate change**.
- Common mistake: ~~tackle the climate~~ / ~~tackle climate changing~~

### 2. emit greenhouse gases
- Weak → better: *produce harmful gases* → **emit greenhouse gases**
- 意思：排放溫室氣體
- Frame: `factories / vehicles / industries + emit greenhouse gases`
- Model: Coal-fired power plants still **emit greenhouse gases** in large amounts.

### 3. adopt eco-friendly practices
- Weak → better: *do green things* → **adopt eco-friendly practices**
- 意思：採用環保做法／習慣
- Frame: `households / businesses / schools + adopt eco-friendly practices`
- Model: More companies are starting to **adopt eco-friendly practices** in their offices.

### 4. cut down on energy consumption
- Weak → better: *use less energy* → **cut down on energy consumption**
- 意思：減少能源消耗
- Frame: `cut down on + energy consumption / waste / plastic use`
- Model: Simple habits at home can help people **cut down on energy consumption**.

### 5. protect endangered species
- Weak → better: *save rare animals* → **protect endangered species**
- 意思：保護瀕危物種
- Frame: `laws / reserves / efforts + protect endangered species`
- Model: National parks play a key role in helping to **protect endangered species**.

## Reading passage

Cities around the world are under pressure to **tackle climate change** before the damage becomes irreversible. Factories and vehicles continue to **emit greenhouse gases**, which trap heat in the atmosphere and threaten fragile habitats. Experts argue that households and businesses must **adopt eco-friendly practices**, from recycling to choosing public transport, if they want meaningful progress. At the same time, governments can encourage people to **cut down on energy consumption** through better building standards and clearer labelling on appliances. These steps also help **protect endangered species** whose homes are shrinking as temperatures rise and coastlines change.
