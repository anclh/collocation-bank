# Lesson 8 — Environment（環境）

Date: 2026-09-18

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. cause irreversible damage to
- Weak: damage nature badly
- 意思：不可逆轉破壞
- Frame: `cause irreversible damage to + noun`
- Model: Deforestation can cause irreversible damage to local ecosystems.
- Mistake: damage 係不可數，唔寫 damages

### 2. deplete natural resources
- Weak: use up natural things
- 意思：耗盡自然資源
- Frame: `deplete natural resources`
- Model: Overconsumption continues to deplete natural resources at an alarming rate.
- Mistake: 唔好用 finish

### 3. reduce carbon emissions
- Weak: cut CO2
- 意思：減少碳排放
- Frame: `reduce/cut carbon emissions`
- Model: Governments must reduce carbon emissions to limit global warming.
- Mistake: emission 用單數通常唔啱

### 4. switch to renewable energy sources
- Weak: use green energy
- 意思：轉用可再生能源
- Frame: `switch to renewable energy sources`
- Model: Cities should switch to renewable energy sources such as solar and wind power.
- Mistake: 較好寫 renewable energy sources

### 5. preserve biodiversity
- Weak: protect animals
- 意思：保護生物多樣性
- Frame: `preserve/protect biodiversity`
- Model: National parks help preserve biodiversity by providing safe habitats for wildlife.
- Mistake: biodiversity 係不可數，唔寫 biodiversities
