# Lesson 5 — Increase, Decrease and Quantity（增減與數量）

Date: 2026-09-15

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. a sharp increase in
- Weak: go up a lot
- 意思：大幅上升
- Frame: `a sharp increase in + noun`
- Model: There has been a sharp increase in remote working since 2020.
- Mistake: increase of people → 用 increase in the number of

### 2. decline significantly
- Weak: go down a lot
- 意思：明顯下降
- Frame: `decline significantly / a significant decline in`
- Model: Birth rates have declined significantly in many developed countries.
- Mistake: decline in sales，唔好寫 decline to do

### 3. a substantial proportion of
- Weak: a lot of
- 意思：相當大比例
- Frame: `a substantial proportion of + noun`
- Model: A substantial proportion of young people rely on public transport.
- Mistake: proportion from → 用 of

### 4. experience a surge in
- Weak: suddenly have much more
- 意思：經歷急升
- Frame: `experience a surge in + noun`
- Model: Online retailers experienced a surge in demand during the holiday season.
- Mistake: 較自然寫 surge in demand

### 5. account for
- Weak: make up
- 意思：佔（比例）
- Frame: `account for + percentage`
- Model: Renewable energy now accounts for nearly 30% of electricity generation.
- Mistake: 呢度係 make up，唔係 explain

## Reading passage


After full reopening of the boundary, Hong Kong recorded **a sharp increase in** short-haul visitor arrivals, filling hotels and cross-boundary transport on peak weekends. Same-day trips from the Greater Bay Area now **account for** a rising share of footfall in core retail districts, while some longer-haul markets still **decline significantly** compared with pre-pandemic peaks. Tourism operators also **experience a surge in** midweek bookings whenever large exhibitions coincide with public holidays. On balance, **a substantial proportion of** the rebound remains concentrated in a few districts, so knock-on effects on quieter neighbourhoods are uneven. Authorities therefore favour diversifying attractions rather than relying on headline arrival totals alone.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊類 trips account for a rising share of footfall？
2. 用自己一句解釋點解 rebound 仍然 uneven（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話所有客源市場都已經超越疫情前高峰。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **Same-day trips from the Greater Bay Area**
2. （例）a large share of the rebound is concentrated in a few districts
3. **False**（some longer-haul markets still decline significantly vs pre-pandemic peaks）
