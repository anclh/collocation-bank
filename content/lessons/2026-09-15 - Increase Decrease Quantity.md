# Lesson 5 — Increase, Decrease and Quantity（增減與數量）

Date: 2026-09-15

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. a sharp increase in
- Weak: go up a lot
- 意思：大幅上升
- Frame: `a sharp increase in + noun`
- Model: There has been a sharp increase in remote working since 2020.
- Mistake: increase of people → 用 increase in the number of

### 2. decline significantly
- Weak: go down a lot
- 意思：明顯下降
- Frame: `decline significantly / a significant decline in`
- Model: Birth rates have declined significantly in many developed countries.
- Mistake: decline in sales，唔好寫 decline to do

### 3. a substantial proportion of
- Weak: a lot of
- 意思：相當大比例
- Frame: `a substantial proportion of + noun`
- Model: A substantial proportion of young people rely on public transport.
- Mistake: proportion from → 用 of

### 4. experience a surge in
- Weak: suddenly have much more
- 意思：經歷急升
- Frame: `experience a surge in + noun`
- Model: Online retailers experienced a surge in demand during the holiday season.
- Mistake: 較自然寫 surge in demand

### 5. account for
- Weak: make up
- 意思：佔（比例）
- Frame: `account for + percentage`
- Model: Renewable energy now accounts for nearly 30% of electricity generation.
- Mistake: 呢度係 make up，唔係 explain

## Reading passage

After the pandemic reopening, several Asian cities recorded **a sharp increase in** weekend tourist arrivals, filling hotels that had stood half-empty for months. At the same time, visits to smaller museums began to **decline significantly** as travellers clustered around a few famous landmarks. Tourism officials note that **a substantial proportion of** spending still comes from domestic visitors rather than overseas guests. Some districts **experience a surge in** short-stay bookings whenever a major concert or sports final is held. Overall, lodging and food services now **account for** a larger share of local tax revenue than before 2020, so planners must balance growth with crowd control and neighbourhood noise.

**Passage tasks**
1. 邊類場所嘅參觀數字 decline significantly？
2. 用自己說話講咩時候會 experience a surge in bookings（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話大部分消費嚟自海外旅客。
