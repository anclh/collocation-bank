# Lesson 4 — Solutions and Measures（解決方案與措施）

Date: 2026-09-14

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. take measures to
- Weak: do something about
- 意思：採取措施
- Frame: `take measures to + V`
- Model: Governments should take measures to reduce plastic waste.
- Mistake: measures of reducing → 用 to reduce

### 2. implement policies
- Weak: put rules into practice
- 意思：落實政策
- Frame: `implement policies (aimed at / to + V)`
- Model: Many cities have implemented policies aimed at cutting traffic emissions.
- Mistake: implement 唔可以接 people

### 3. address the issue
- Weak: deal with the problem
- 意思：處理問題
- Frame: `address the issue/problem of + N`
- Model: Schools need to address the issue of exam stress among students.
- Mistake: 呢度 address 唔係街道地址

### 4. mitigate the effects of
- Weak: make bad effects smaller
- 意思：減輕影響
- Frame: `mitigate the effects/impact of + N`
- Model: Early intervention can mitigate the effects of childhood poverty.
- Mistake: 留意 mitigate 嘅拼法

### 5. put forward proposals
- Weak: suggest ideas
- 意思：提出方案
- Frame: `put forward proposals / a proposal to + V`
- Model: Experts have put forward proposals to improve public transport.
- Mistake: put forwards → 唔加 s
