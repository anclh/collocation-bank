# Lesson 14 — Cause and Result（因果）

Date: 2026-09-28

## Quick review（Lesson 13）

1. **have far-reaching consequences for** — 對……帶來深遠／廣泛後果
2. **exert a strong influence on** — 對……施加強烈影響
3. **take its toll on** — 逐漸對……造成損害／損耗

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. arise from
- Weak: *come from / happen because of*
- 意思：源於／由……引起（正式）
- Frame: `X arises / arise from + noun`
- Model: Many workplace conflicts **arise from** poor communication.
- Mistake: 主語多數係問題／情況；唔好寫 *arise from that people…*（後面跟 noun / noun phrase）

### 2. be the root cause of
- Weak: *be the main reason for*
- 意思：係……嘅根本原因
- Frame: `X is / are the root cause of + noun`
- Model: Poverty is often **the root cause of** poor educational outcomes.
- Mistake: 用 *the root cause*（通常單數）；後面 *of*，唔係 *for*

### 3. culminate in
- Weak: *finally cause / end up causing*
- 意思：最終發展成為／以……告終
- Frame: `X culminates / culminated in + noun`
- Model: Years of underinvestment **culminated in** a serious housing shortage.
- Mistake: 後面用 *in*；通常指一段過程嘅最終結果

### 4. be largely due to
- Weak: *mainly because of*
- 意思：主要由於……
- Frame: `X is / was largely due to + noun`
- Model: The drop in sales **was largely due to** weaker consumer demand.
- Mistake: *due to* 後面跟 noun；正式寫作避免 *due to people spent less*

### 5. be a direct consequence of
- Weak: *be directly caused by*
- 意思：係……嘅直接後果
- Frame: `X is / are a direct consequence of + noun`
- Model: Rising youth unemployment **is a direct consequence of** slow economic growth.
- Mistake: 用 *consequence of*；*direct* 強調因果好明顯

## Reading passage


Roadside pollution spikes in Hong Kong’s older urban corridors often **arise from** a mix of freight traffic, canyon-like street design, and weather that traps exhaust. Idling and ageing diesel fleets are frequently **the root cause of** local exceedances even when regional background levels look moderate. Prolonged exposure can **culminate in** higher clinic visits for respiratory complaints among children and elders. Recent improvement in some monitoring stations is **largely due to** tighter vehicle standards and franchised-bus replacement programmes. Extra hospital burden during haze episodes is **a direct consequence of** days when both local emissions and regional factors align—hence the case for phased low-emission zones subject to stakeholder consultation.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊啲因素經常係 local exceedances 嘅 root cause？
2. 用自己一句解釋近期部分監測站改善 largely due to 咩（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話 roadside pollution 只係因為區域因素，同本地車隊無關。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **Idling** and **ageing diesel fleets**
2. （例）tighter vehicle standards and franchised-bus replacement programmes
3. **False**（arise from freight traffic / street design / local fleets as well）
