# Lesson 3 — Problems and Threats（問題與威脅）

Date: 2026-09-13

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. pose a threat to
- Weak: be dangerous for
- 意思：對……構成威脅
- Frame: `pose a (serious) threat to + N`
- Model: Rising sea levels pose a serious threat to coastal cities.
- Mistake: threat for → 用 to

### 2. raise concerns about
- Weak: make people worry
- 意思：引起關注／憂慮
- Frame: `raise concerns about + N/V-ing`
- Model: The new policy has raised concerns about data privacy.
- Mistake: concerns of → 用 about

### 3. be faced with
- Weak: have a problem
- 意思：面對
- Frame: `be faced with + N`
- Model: Many graduates are faced with intense competition in the job market.
- Mistake: faced by → 用 with

### 4. exacerbate the problem
- Weak: make worse
- 意思：令問題惡化
- Frame: `exacerbate the problem/situation`
- Model: Cutting education funding may exacerbate the problem of youth unemployment.
- Mistake: exacerbate 唔等於 exasperate

### 5. be a major concern
- Weak: big worry
- 意思：重大關注點
- Frame: `be a major concern for/among`
- Model: Housing affordability is a major concern for young adults in Hong Kong.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 a major concerning
