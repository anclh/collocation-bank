# Lesson 2 — Cause and Result（原因與結果）

Date: 2026-09-12

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. lead to
- Weak: cause/make
- 意思：導致
- Frame: `X leads to Y / lead to + V-ing`
- Model: Heavy traffic often leads to longer commuting times.
- Mistake: lead to rise → lead to rising costs

### 2. result in
- Weak: cause/bring
- 意思：造成結果
- Frame: `X results in Y`
- Model: Poor diet can result in serious health problems.
- Mistake: 唔好同 result from 混淆

### 3. stem from
- Weak: come from/because of
- 意思：源自
- Frame: `X stems from Y`
- Model: Many social tensions stem from economic inequality.
- Mistake: stem from because 係重複

### 4. give rise to
- Weak: cause/create
- 意思：引發
- Frame: `X gives rise to Y`
- Model: Rapid urbanisation has given rise to housing shortages.
- Mistake: 通常唔寫 I give rise to

### 5. be attributed to
- Weak: because of
- 意思：被歸因於
- Frame: `X is/can be attributed to Y`
- Model: The decline in reading can be attributed to excessive screen time.
- Mistake: 較自然用被動 be attributed to
