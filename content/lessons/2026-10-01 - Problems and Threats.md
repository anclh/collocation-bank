# Lesson 15 — Problems and Threats（問題與威脅）

Date: 2026-10-01

## Quick review（Lesson 14）

1. **arise from** — 源於／由……引起
2. **be largely due to** — 主要由於……
3. **be a direct consequence of** — 係……嘅直接後果

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. be plagued by
- Weak: *have a lot of problems with / keep suffering from*
- 意思：長期飽受……困擾（問題／麻煩持續出現）
- Frame: `X is / are plagued by + noun`
- Model: Many coastal cities **are plagued by** flooding during the rainy season.
- Mistake: 後面通常跟問題／負面情況（crime, delays, shortages），唔係正面事物

### 2. be under threat
- Weak: *might be damaged / could be lost*
- 意思：正面臨威脅／岌岌可危
- Frame: `X is / are under threat (from + noun)`
- Model: Traditional crafts **are under threat** from cheap mass production.
- Mistake: 用 *under threat*（唔係 *under threaten*）；可加 *from* 說明來源

### 3. run the risk of
- Weak: *might / there is a chance that*
- 意思：冒住……嘅風險
- Frame: `run the risk of + -ing / noun`
- Model: Governments that ignore youth unemployment **run the risk of** long-term social unrest.
- Mistake: 後面多數跟 *-ing* 或名詞；唔好寫 *run the risk to do*

### 4. be vulnerable to
- Weak: *easily hurt by / easily affected by*
- 意思：容易受到……傷害／影響（較弱勢、抵禦力低）
- Frame: `X is / are vulnerable to + noun`
- Model: Low-income families **are vulnerable to** sudden rises in food prices.
- Mistake: 用 *vulnerable to*（唔係 *vulnerable of / for*）

### 5. present a formidable challenge
- Weak: *be a very hard problem*
- 意思：構成重大／艱巨嘅挑戰
- Frame: `X presents / present a formidable challenge (to + noun)`
- Model: An ageing population **presents a formidable challenge** to healthcare systems.
- Mistake: *formidable* 係「艱巨」；後面可用 *to* 指出對邊方面有挑戰


## Reading passage

**Prompt:** Why some cities struggle with housing shortages.

Many large cities **are plagued by** severe housing shortages, which push rents higher and force young workers to live far from their jobs. Affordable flats **are under threat** as older neighbourhoods are redeveloped into luxury projects that long-term residents cannot afford. Families who rent **are vulnerable to** sudden eviction or sharp rent increases, especially when contracts offer little protection. Policymakers who delay action **run the risk of** deeper inequality, longer commuting times, and weaker social trust. Overall, balancing growth with fair housing access **presents a formidable challenge** for urban planners and local governments, who must weigh developer interests against community stability.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中話 affordable flats 點解 are under threat？
2. 用自己說話講 delay action 會 run the risk of 咩（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話 housing 問題對社會信任冇影響。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **redeveloped into luxury projects**
2.（例）deeper inequality / longer commutes / weaker trust
3. **False**（mentions weaker social trust）
