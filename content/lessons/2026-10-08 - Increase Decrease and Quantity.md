# Lesson 17 — 2026-10-08 — Increase, Decrease and Quantity

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. a growing number of
- Weak: more and more people → Better: a growing number of people
- 意思：越嚟越多嘅…（強調數量持續增加嘅趨勢）
- Frame: A growing number of + plural noun + plural verb
- Model: A growing number of young people are choosing to work remotely.
- Common mistake: ✗ A growing number of students is… → ✓ are

### 2. rise steadily
- Weak: go up slowly → Better: rise steadily
- 意思：穩定、持續咁上升
- Frame: sth + rose steadily + from X to Y / over the period
- Model: The number of tourists rose steadily from 2 million in 2010 to 5 million in 2020.
- Common mistake: ✗ raised steadily（raise 要有受詞）→ ✓ rose steadily

### 3. a dramatic fall in
- Weak: go down a lot → Better: there was a dramatic fall in
- 意思：大幅下跌
- Frame: there was a dramatic fall in + noun
- Model: There was a dramatic fall in car sales during the economic crisis.
- Common mistake: ✗ a dramatic fall of sales → ✓ a dramatic fall in sales

### 4. reach a peak of
- Weak: get to the highest point → Better: reach a peak of
- 意思：去到最高峰
- Frame: sth + reached a peak of + number + in + year
- Model: Unemployment reached a peak of 8% in 2009.
- Common mistake: ✗ reached to a peak / ✗ reached a peak at 8% → ✓ reached a peak of 8%

### 5. level off
- Weak: stop changing → Better: level off (at)
- 意思：升完或跌完之後趨於平穩
- Frame: sth + levelled off + at + number
- Model: After years of rapid growth, the city's population levelled off at around 7.5 million.
- Common mistake: ✗ levelled off to 7.5 million → ✓ levelled off at; ✗ was levelled off（唔用被動）

## Reading passage


**A growing number of** public rental blocks now take part in food-waste collection pilots linked to Green@Community and downstream treatment. Participation rates **rose steadily** after estate-level education drives, then appeared to **level off** once early adopters were already enrolled. During the pandemic years the city saw **a dramatic fall in** commercial food waste from restaurants, before volumes recovered with dine-in demand. Monthly diversion briefly **reached a peak** after major awareness campaigns, illustrating how take-up can spike and then soften without ongoing outreach. On balance, scaling smart bins remains worthwhile, yet recurrent expenditure and contamination rates still warrant careful review.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中 participation rates 喺早期教育後有咩變化，之後又點？
2. 用自己一句解釋疫情期間 commercial food waste 點解有 dramatic fall（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話 diversion 峰值之後就永遠唔會回落。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **rose steadily**, then appeared to **level off**
2. （例）restaurant dine-in demand dropped, so commercial food waste fell sharply
3. **False**（can spike and then soften without ongoing outreach）
