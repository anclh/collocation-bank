# 2026-11-08 — Cause and result (Lesson 26)

## Quick review（Lesson 25）

1. **have far-reaching consequences for** — 對……造成深遠影響
2. **have a knock-on effect on** — 對……產生連鎖影響
3. **make a significant difference to** — 對……帶來顯著改變

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. stem from
- Weak → better: *come from / be because of* → **stem from**
- 意思：源自……；由……引起
- Frame: `stem from + a cause / problem / decision`
- Model: Unequal access to career advice may **stem from** differences in school resources.
- Common mistake: ~~stem from of~~（*from* 已經係介詞，後面直接接名詞）

### 2. contribute to
- Weak → better: *help cause* → **contribute to**
- 意思：促成／導致（某個結果，未必係唯一原因）
- Frame: `contribute to + noun / -ing`
- Model: Practical work placements can **contribute to** a smoother transition from school to employment.
- Common mistake: ~~contribute for~~（表示「促成」時用 *to*）

### 3. set off a chain reaction
- Weak → better: *cause many connected changes* → **set off a chain reaction**
- 意思：引發一連串互相關連嘅變化
- Frame: `set off a chain reaction of + noun`
- Model: A well-matched internship can **set off a chain reaction of** new skills, contacts and job opportunities.
- Common mistake: ~~set out a chain reaction~~（固定搭配係 *set off*，即「引發」）

### 4. pave the way for
- Weak → better: *make it possible for* → **pave the way for**
- 意思：為……鋪路；創造條件令……更容易發生
- Frame: `pave the way for + noun / someone to + verb`
- Model: Early exposure to different industries can **pave the way for** better-informed career choices.
- Common mistake: ~~pave the way to young people~~（後面接名詞用 *for*；接地方先用 *to*）

### 5. culminate in
- Weak → better: *end with / finally lead to* → **culminate in**
- 意思：最終以……告終／發展成……
- Frame: `culminate in + noun / -ing`
- Model: Years of training and workplace experience may **culminate in** a stable career.
- Common mistake: ~~culminate to~~（結果前面用 *in*）

## Mini drill

**A.** 填空：A skills gap may ________ ________ differences in access to training.
**B.** 選正確介詞：(a) contribute **for** a stronger workforce / (b) contribute **to** a stronger workforce
**C.** 改錯：The programme culminated to a series of paid placements.

## Reading passage

At the Youth Development Summit on 7 November, Hong Kong's Chief Secretary said the Youth Development Blueprint had implemented nearly 300 initiatives in four years. This work **stems from** policy efforts to link young people's development with the city's economic needs. The latest Policy Address also prioritises youth development and expands internships and training in areas such as finance, aerospace technology, research and AI. By gaining practical experience, participants can **contribute to** a smoother school-to-work transition; successful placements may then **set off a chain reaction of** new skills, employer contacts and further opportunities. Fair access and clear guidance can **pave the way for** more young people to benefit. With regular evaluation, these connected measures may **culminate in** stronger career prospects and a more adaptable workforce.

**Passage tasks**
1. What is one reason given for connecting youth development with economic needs?
2. According to the passage, name two possible links in the chain reaction after a successful placement.
3. What two conditions could help more young people benefit from the measures?

## ANSWER KEY

### Mini drill
- A: **stem from**
- B: **(b)** contribute **to** a stronger workforce
- C: The programme culminated **in** a series of paid placements.

### Passage tasks
1. To link young people's development with the city's economic needs.
2. New skills, employer contacts or further opportunities (any two).
3. Fair access and clear guidance.
