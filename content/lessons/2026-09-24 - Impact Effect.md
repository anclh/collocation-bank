# Lesson 13 — Impact / Effect（影響／效果）

Date: 2026-09-24

## Quick review（Lesson 12）

1. **play a vital role in** — 喺某事入面扮演關鍵角色
2. **be of great importance to** — 對……極為重要
3. **be essential to** — 對……必不可少

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. have far-reaching consequences for
- Weak: *have a big effect on many things*
- 意思：對……帶來深遠／廣泛後果
- Frame: `X has / have far-reaching consequences for + noun`
- Model: Air pollution **has far-reaching consequences for** public health and the economy.
- Mistake: 用複數 *consequences*；前置詞用 *for*，唔係 *to*

### 2. exert a strong influence on
- Weak: *strongly affect / have a big effect on*
- 意思：對……施加強烈影響
- Frame: `X exerts a strong influence on + noun`
- Model: Social media **exerts a strong influence on** young people’s opinions.
- Mistake: 動詞係 *exert*；後面用 *on*

### 3. bring about change
- Weak: *cause change / make things change*
- 意思：促成／導致改變（正式）
- Frame: `X brings / brought about + (a) change / noun`
- Model: New transport policies **brought about** a clear **change** in commuting habits.
- Mistake: 完整片語係 *bring about*；唔好寫 *bring a change about of*

### 4. have a knock-on effect on
- Weak: *then affect other things too*
- 意思：產生連鎖／連帶影響
- Frame: `X has / have a knock-on effect on + noun`
- Model: Rising fuel prices **have a knock-on effect on** food costs.
- Mistake: 用 *knock-on effect*；後面 *on*

### 5. take its toll on
- Weak: *slowly damage / hurt over time*
- 意思：逐漸對……造成損害／損耗
- Frame: `X takes / took its toll on + noun`
- Model: Long working hours **take their toll on** employees’ mental health.
- Mistake: 主語複數時用 *take their toll*；後面用 *on*


## Reading passage


Elevated private rents in Hong Kong’s urban districts **have far-reaching consequences for** household budgets, childcare choices, and even whether dual-career couples delay starting a family. Housing costs also **exert a strong influence on** labour mobility, as workers hesitate to take jobs far from cheap accommodation. Light Public Housing and transitional projects seek to **bring about change** for long-waiting applicants without claiming to reset the whole market overnight. When more households free up subdivided units, the shift can **have a knock-on effect on** neighbourhood retail and school places. Persistent stress over rent, however, continues to **take its toll on** mental well-being among caregivers and young tenants alike.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中 housing costs exert a strong influence on 咩？
2. 用自己一句解釋 Light Public Housing 點樣 bring about change（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話租金壓力對 mental well-being 冇影響。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **labour mobility**（workers hesitate to take distant jobs）
2. （例）it offers interim homes for long-waiting applicants without resetting the whole market at once
3. **False**（continues to take its toll on mental well-being）
