## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. play a pivotal role in
- Weak → better: *be very important in / be the main part of* → **play a pivotal role in**
- 意思：喺……入面發揮關鍵／樞紐作用
- Frame: `play a pivotal role in + noun / -ing`
- Model: District Health Centres **play a pivotal role in** shifting routine care away from crowded hospital wards.
- Common mistake: ~~play a pivotal role on~~（介詞用 *in*，唔係 *on*）

### 2. be of paramount importance
- Weak → better: *be the most important / be extremely important* → **be of paramount importance**
- 意思：極為重要；至關重要
- Frame: `be of paramount importance (to / for + noun); It is of paramount importance that…`
- Model: Reliable waiting-time data **is of paramount importance** when families choose a clinic or specialist pathway.
- Common mistake: ~~be of paramount important~~（要用名詞 *importance*，唔係 *important*）

### 3. serve as a cornerstone of
- Weak → better: *be the basic support of / be the foundation of* → **serve as a cornerstone of**
- 意思：作為……嘅基石／支柱
- Frame: `serve as a cornerstone of + system / strategy / policy`
- Model: Community nursing now **serves as a cornerstone of** Hong Kong's primary healthcare strategy.
- Common mistake: ~~serve as a corner stone for~~（寫成一個字 *cornerstone*；後面多用 *of*）

### 4. lie at the heart of
- Weak → better: *be the central idea of / be the core of* → **lie at the heart of**
- 意思：處於……嘅核心；係……最關鍵嘅部分
- Frame: `lie at the heart of + plan / debate / reform`
- Model: Prevention and early detection **lie at the heart of** the Hospital Authority's chronic-disease programmes.
- Common mistake: ~~lie in the heart of~~（固定係 *at the heart of*）

### 5. be indispensable to
- Weak → better: *be needed a lot by / cannot work without* → **be indispensable to**
- 意思：對……不可或缺
- Frame: `be indispensable to + noun / -ing; an indispensable part of`
- Model: Cross-bureau coordination **is indispensable to** reducing avoidable accident-and-emergency attendances.
- Common mistake: ~~be indispensable for to~~（介詞用 *to*；或者改寫 *indispensable for + noun*，唔好兩個一齊用）

## Mini drill

**A.** 填空（今日 5 個搭配）：Prevention and early detection ________ ________ ________ ________ the chronic-disease programmes.
**B.** 選較好一句：(a) eHealth records are of paramount important. / (b) eHealth records are of paramount importance.
**C.** 改錯：District clinics play a pivotal role on primary care.

## Reading passage

Hong Kong's primary healthcare reform puts neighbourhood clinics closer to where people live and work. District Health Centres now **play a pivotal role in** catching hypertension and diabetes early, before patients need a hospital bed. For that shift to work, shared eHealth records **are of paramount importance**: without them, family doctors and specialists cannot see the same test history. Community nursing **serves as a cornerstone of** follow-up care after discharge, cutting repeat visits that strain the fiscal envelope. In practice, prevention and lifestyle advice **lie at the heart of** these programmes, not only medicine after a crisis. Smooth data-sharing across the Hospital Authority and private providers **is indispensable to** closing the remaining service gap and easing pressure on accident-and-emergency departments.

**Passage tasks**
1. List the five taught collocations used in the passage.
2. In one sentence, why are shared eHealth records described as so important?
3. Match: District Health Centres → ? ; community nursing → ? ; prevention advice → ?（選：pivotal role / cornerstone / heart of the programmes）
