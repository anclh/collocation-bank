# Lesson 9 — Technology（科技）

Date: 2026-09-19

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. rely heavily on technology
- Weak: depend a lot on tech
- 意思：好依賴科技
- Frame: `rely heavily on technology`
- Model: Many industries now rely heavily on technology to stay competitive.
- Mistake: heavily in → 用 on

### 2. keep pace with technological advances
- Weak: keep up with new tech
- 意思：跟得上科技進步
- Frame: `keep pace with technological advances`
- Model: Schools must keep pace with technological advances to prepare students for the future.
- Mistake: pace to → 用 with

### 3. bridge the digital divide
- Weak: make internet gap smaller
- 意思：縮短數碼鴻溝
- Frame: `bridge the digital divide (between…)`
- Model: Governments should invest in infrastructure to bridge the digital divide between urban and rural areas.
- Mistake: 要用 the

### 4. raise privacy concerns
- Weak: make people worry about privacy
- 意思：引起私隱憂慮
- Frame: `raise privacy concerns`
- Model: Facial-recognition cameras in public places raise privacy concerns among citizens.
- Mistake: concerning → 用 concerns

### 5. transform the way we …
- Weak: change how we
- 意思：徹底改變方式
- Frame: `transform the way we work/learn/communicate`
- Model: Smartphones have transformed the way we communicate with friends and colleagues.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 the way how we

## Reading passage


Public services in Hong Kong now **rely heavily on technology** for licensing, booking, and benefit applications, so outages quickly disrupt daily life. Bureaux must **keep pace with technological advances** if digital government channels are to remain usable as private-sector apps raise expectations. Targeted support for elders and low-income households is meant to **bridge the digital divide**, yet new data-sharing pilots still **raise privacy concerns** among civil-society groups. On balance, thoughtful design can **transform the way we** access hospital appointments and community services without excluding those offline. Stakeholder consultation and clear opt-outs therefore sit alongside the push for higher take-up.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中點樣嘗試 bridge the digital divide？
2. 用自己一句解釋點解 data-sharing pilots raise privacy concerns（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話數碼化可以完全唔理 offline users。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **Targeted support for elders and low-income households**
2. （例）civil-society groups worry about how personal data may be shared in new pilots
3. **False**（without excluding those offline；要 consultation／opt-outs）
