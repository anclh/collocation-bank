# Lesson 9 — Technology（科技）

Date: 2026-09-19

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. rely heavily on technology
- Weak: depend a lot on tech
- 意思：好依賴科技
- Frame: `rely heavily on technology`
- Model: Many industries now rely heavily on technology to stay competitive.
- Mistake: heavily in → 用 on

### 2. keep pace with technological advances
- Weak: keep up with new tech
- 意思：跟得上科技進步
- Frame: `keep pace with technological advances`
- Model: Schools must keep pace with technological advances to prepare students for the future.
- Mistake: pace to → 用 with

### 3. bridge the digital divide
- Weak: make internet gap smaller
- 意思：縮短數碼鴻溝
- Frame: `bridge the digital divide (between…)`
- Model: Governments should invest in infrastructure to bridge the digital divide between urban and rural areas.
- Mistake: 要用 the

### 4. raise privacy concerns
- Weak: make people worry about privacy
- 意思：引起私隱憂慮
- Frame: `raise privacy concerns`
- Model: Facial-recognition cameras in public places raise privacy concerns among citizens.
- Mistake: concerning → 用 concerns

### 5. transform the way we …
- Weak: change how we
- 意思：徹底改變方式
- Frame: `transform the way we work/learn/communicate`
- Model: Smartphones have transformed the way we communicate with friends and colleagues.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 the way how we

## Reading passage

Modern workplaces **rely heavily on technology** for meetings, records, and customer service, so outages quickly disrupt entire teams. Employees must **keep pace with technological advances**, updating skills as software and AI tools change each year. At the same time, policymakers try to **bridge the digital divide** between well-connected cities and villages with weak broadband. New tracking apps **raise privacy concerns** when personal data is stored without clear consent. Used wisely, the same innovations can **transform the way we** learn, shop, and access public services, but only if clear rules protect users while still encouraging useful experimentation in schools and workplaces.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中點解話要 keep pace with technological advances？
2. 用自己說話解釋 digital divide 係指咩（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話科技轉變必然無隱私問題。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **software and AI tools change each year** / skills need updating
2.（例）gap between well-connected cities and poorly connected villages
3. **False**（raise privacy concerns）
