## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. play a vital role in
- Weak: *is very important*
- 意思：喺某事入面扮演關鍵角色
- Frame: `X plays a vital role in + noun / V-ing`
- Model: Teachers **play a vital role in** shaping young people’s values.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 *play a vital role to do*；用 *in + noun/V-ing*

### 2. be of great importance to
- Weak: *is very important for*
- 意思：對……極為重要（正式學術語氣）
- Frame: `X is / are of great importance to + noun`
- Model: Reliable data **is of great importance to** policymakers.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 *of greatly importance*；用形容詞 *great*

### 3. be essential to
- Weak: *is necessary / needed*
- 意思：對……必不可少
- Frame: `X is essential to + noun / V-ing`
- Model: Clear communication **is essential to** teamwork.
- Mistake: 後面多數接名詞或 V-ing

### 4. be instrumental in
- Weak: *helped a lot to*
- 意思：對促成某事起重要作用
- Frame: `X was / is instrumental in + V-ing / noun`
- Model: Community groups **were instrumental in** raising awareness of the issue.
- Mistake: 用 *in + V-ing*，唔係 *instrumental to do*

### 5. occupy a central place in
- Weak: *is the most important part of*
- 意思：喺……當中佔核心位置
- Frame: `X occupies a central place in + noun`
- Model: Education **occupies a central place in** national development plans.
- Mistake: *place* 多數用單數


## Reading passage


Primary care now **occupies a central place in** Hong Kong’s strategy for an ageing population. District Health Centres **play a vital role in** diverting stable cases away from specialist queues, while eHealth records **are of great importance to** continuity when patients move between clinics. Reliable family-medicine slots **are essential to** catching chronic disease early, and nurse-led follow-up has **been instrumental in** raising vaccination and screening take-up in several districts. On balance, community networks that link social and medical support remain as critical as hospital expansion if the system is to stay sustainable within the fiscal envelope.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊啲 centres play a vital role in diverting stable cases？
2. 用自己一句解釋 eHealth records 點解 are of great importance（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話只靠加醫院床位就夠應付人口老化。

2. （例）they support continuity when patients move between clinics
