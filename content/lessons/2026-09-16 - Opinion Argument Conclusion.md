# Lesson 6 — Opinion, Argument and Conclusion（觀點、論據與結論）

Date: 2026-09-16

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. hold the view that
- Weak: I think
- 意思：認為
- Frame: `hold the view that + clause`
- Model: Many educators hold the view that creativity should be assessed alongside exam scores.
- Mistake: hold the view of that → 用 hold the view that

### 2. it is widely believed that
- Weak: many people think
- 意思：普遍認為
- Frame: `It is widely believed that + clause`
- Model: It is widely believed that early reading habits shape later academic success.
- Mistake: widely believe → 用 widely believed

### 3. lend support to
- Weak: support
- 意思：為論點提供支持
- Frame: `lend support to + noun`
- Model: Recent surveys lend support to the argument that flexible hours raise productivity.
- Mistake: support for → 呢個片語用 to

### 4. cast doubt on
- Weak: make people doubt
- 意思：令人質疑
- Frame: `cast doubt on + noun`
- Model: Conflicting data cast doubt on the claim that longer school days always help.
- Mistake: doubt to → 用 on

### 5. reach the conclusion that
- Weak: so I think
- 意思：得出結論
- Frame: `reach the conclusion that + clause`
- Model: Overall, I reach the conclusion that balanced assessment benefits both students and teachers.
- Mistake: reach to the conclusion → 唔用 to

## Reading passage


Many carers **hold the view that** community care vouchers offer more dignity than immediate residential placement for frail elders who still have family support. **It is widely believed that** expanding voucher take-up can ease pressure on residential waiting lists, and early evaluation reports **lend support to** that reading where day-care slots are available. Critics nonetheless **cast doubt on** whether the current fiscal envelope can keep pace if demand rises sharply among dual-income households. After weighing service gaps and caregiver strain, district welfare teams often **reach the conclusion that** a mixed model—vouchers plus targeted residential places—remains preferable on balance to a single-track approach.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊啲報告 lend support to expanding voucher take-up？
2. 用自己一句解釋 critic 點解 cast doubt on the fiscal envelope（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 最終 favour 單一 residential-only 方案。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **early evaluation reports**（where day-care slots are available）
2. （例）demand from dual-income households may grow faster than the budget can support
3. **False**（mixed model … preferable on balance）
