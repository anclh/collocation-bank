# Lesson 6 — Opinion, Argument and Conclusion（觀點、論據與結論）

Date: 2026-09-16

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. hold the view that
- Weak: I think
- 意思：認為
- Frame: `hold the view that + clause`
- Model: Many educators hold the view that creativity should be assessed alongside exam scores.
- Mistake: hold the view of that → 用 hold the view that

### 2. it is widely believed that
- Weak: many people think
- 意思：普遍認為
- Frame: `It is widely believed that + clause`
- Model: It is widely believed that early reading habits shape later academic success.
- Mistake: widely believe → 用 widely believed

### 3. lend support to
- Weak: support
- 意思：為論點提供支持
- Frame: `lend support to + noun`
- Model: Recent surveys lend support to the argument that flexible hours raise productivity.
- Mistake: support for → 呢個片語用 to

### 4. cast doubt on
- Weak: make people doubt
- 意思：令人質疑
- Frame: `cast doubt on + noun`
- Model: Conflicting data cast doubt on the claim that longer school days always help.
- Mistake: doubt to → 用 on

### 5. reach the conclusion that
- Weak: so I think
- 意思：得出結論
- Frame: `reach the conclusion that + clause`
- Model: Overall, I reach the conclusion that balanced assessment benefits both students and teachers.
- Mistake: reach to the conclusion → 唔用 to
