# Lesson 10 — Health（健康）

Date: 2026-09-20

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. lead a sedentary lifestyle
- Weak: sit a lot
- 意思：少郁動生活
- Frame: `lead a sedentary lifestyle`
- Model: Many office workers lead a sedentary lifestyle, which can weaken their physical fitness over time.
- Mistake: have a sedentary life 冇咁自然

### 2. suffer from chronic diseases
- Weak: have long-term illness
- 意思：患慢性病
- Frame: `suffer from chronic diseases`
- Model: An ageing population means more people suffer from chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease.
- Mistake: 要用 from

### 3. have access to healthcare
- Weak: can get medical help
- 意思：獲得醫療服務
- Frame: `have/gain access to healthcare`
- Model: In rural areas, residents often do not have access to healthcare of the same quality as city dwellers.
- Mistake: healthcare 係不可數，唔寫 healthcares

### 4. improve public health
- Weak: make people’s health better
- 意思：改善公共衛生
- Frame: `improve public health`
- Model: Clean water and vaccination programmes help governments improve public health across the country.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 improve the public’s healthy

### 5. enjoy a longer life expectancy
- Weak: live longer on average
- 意思：更長預期壽命
- Frame: `enjoy a longer/higher life expectancy`
- Model: Thanks to better nutrition and medicine, many countries now enjoy a longer life expectancy than a century ago.
- Mistake: 講國家趨勢通常用單數

## Reading passage


Office workers who **lead a sedentary lifestyle** between long MTR rides and desk shifts are more likely over time to **suffer from chronic diseases** such as hypertension. District Health Centres aim to ensure more residents **have access to healthcare** closer to home before conditions worsen, complementing Hospital Authority clinics. Screening, vaccination, and lifestyle advice are framed as practical ways to **improve public health** within a constrained recurrent expenditure envelope. If prevention takes hold, more people may **enjoy a longer life expectancy** with fewer years spent in avoidable hospital care. On balance, primary-care expansion remains subject to staffing and steady eHealth registration rates.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊類設施幫助居民 have access to healthcare closer to home？
2. 用自己一句解釋 sedentary lifestyle 同 chronic diseases 嘅關係（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話 prevention 唔使理 staffing 同 eHealth take-up。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **District Health Centres**（complementing HA clinics）
2. （例）long hours of sitting raise the chance of conditions such as hypertension
3. **False**（subject to staffing and steady eHealth registration rates）
