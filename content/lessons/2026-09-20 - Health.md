# Lesson 10 — Health（健康）

Date: 2026-09-20

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. lead a sedentary lifestyle
- Weak: sit a lot
- 意思：少郁動生活
- Frame: `lead a sedentary lifestyle`
- Model: Many office workers lead a sedentary lifestyle, which can weaken their physical fitness over time.
- Mistake: have a sedentary life 冇咁自然

### 2. suffer from chronic diseases
- Weak: have long-term illness
- 意思：患慢性病
- Frame: `suffer from chronic diseases`
- Model: An ageing population means more people suffer from chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease.
- Mistake: 要用 from

### 3. have access to healthcare
- Weak: can get medical help
- 意思：獲得醫療服務
- Frame: `have/gain access to healthcare`
- Model: In rural areas, residents often do not have access to healthcare of the same quality as city dwellers.
- Mistake: healthcare 係不可數，唔寫 healthcares

### 4. improve public health
- Weak: make people’s health better
- 意思：改善公共衛生
- Frame: `improve public health`
- Model: Clean water and vaccination programmes help governments improve public health across the country.
- Mistake: 唔好寫 improve the public’s healthy

### 5. enjoy a longer life expectancy
- Weak: live longer on average
- 意思：更長預期壽命
- Frame: `enjoy a longer/higher life expectancy`
- Model: Thanks to better nutrition and medicine, many countries now enjoy a longer life expectancy than a century ago.
- Mistake: 講國家趨勢通常用單數

## Reading passage

Office workers who **lead a sedentary lifestyle** often sit for more than eight hours a day, with little walking between meetings. Over time, many begin to **suffer from chronic diseases** such as type 2 diabetes and heart conditions linked to inactivity. Whether people recover early depends partly on whether they **have access to healthcare** that offers screening and advice, not only emergency treatment. City programmes that promote parks, cycling, and workplace stretching aim to **improve public health** at population level. If these habits take root, more residents may **enjoy a longer life expectancy** without spending their later years in frequent hospital care.

**Passage tasks**
1. Sedentary lifestyle 同邊啲 chronic diseases 有關？
2. 用自己說話解釋 access to healthcare 點樣幫到早期介入（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話 improve public health 只靠醫院急救就夠。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **type 2 diabetes and heart conditions**
2.（例）screening and advice, not only emergencies
3. **False**（parks, cycling, workplace stretching matter too）
