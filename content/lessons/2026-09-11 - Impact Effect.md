# Lesson 1 — Impact / Effect（影響／效果）

Date: 2026-09-11

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. have a significant impact on
- Weak: affect a lot
- 意思：對……有重大影響
- Frame: `X has a significant impact on Y`
- Model: Online learning has a significant impact on students' study habits.
- Mistake: impact to → 用 on

### 2. have a profound impact on
- Weak: deep/strong effect
- 意思：對……有深遠影響
- Frame: `X has a profound impact on Y`
- Model: Childhood education has a profound impact on a person's future development.
- Mistake: profound 唔適合形容輕微影響

### 3. have a detrimental / adverse effect on
- Weak: bad for
- 意思：對……有不良影響
- Frame: `X has a detrimental effect on Y`
- Model: Excessive screen time can have a detrimental effect on teenagers' sleep quality.
- Mistake: 避免寫 bad effect on

### 4. make a significant difference to
- Weak: help a lot
- 意思：對……有明顯分別
- Frame: `X can make a significant difference to Y`
- Model: Small lifestyle changes can make a significant difference to public health.
- Mistake: difference on → 用 to

### 5. contribute to
- Weak: help cause
- 意思：促成／導致
- Frame: `X contributes to Y`
- Model: Poor diet contributes to a wide range of health problems.
- Mistake: 呢度唔只係 donate money
