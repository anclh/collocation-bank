# 2026-10-22 — Technology (Lesson 21)

## Quick review（Lesson 20）

1. **tackle climate change** — 認真處理氣候變化
2. **emit greenhouse gases** — 排放溫室氣體
3. **adopt eco-friendly practices** — 採用環保做法

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. harness the power of
- Weak → better: *use the power of / make good use of* → **harness the power of**
- 意思：善用／駕馭……嘅力量（常配 AI、technology、data）
- Frame: `harness the power of + AI / technology / data / the internet`
- Model: Schools are learning to **harness the power of** AI to personalise lessons.
- Common mistake: ~~harness on the power~~ / ~~harness power of~~（要有 *the*）

### 2. become obsolete
- Weak → better: *become old / out of date* → **become obsolete**
- 意思：變得過時／被淘汰
- Frame: `skills / machines / software + become obsolete`
- Model: Without lifelong learning, some job skills may **become obsolete**.
- Common mistake: ~~become obsolute~~；形容詞係 *obsolete*，唔係 ~~obsoleted~~ 當普通過去式亂用

### 3. enhance productivity
- Weak → better: *make people work better / improve work* → **enhance productivity**
- 意思：提高生產力／工作效率
- Frame: `tools / training / flexible hours + enhance productivity`
- Model: Remote-work software can **enhance productivity** when teams communicate clearly.
- Common mistake: ~~enhance the productivity of work~~（多餘）；較自然係 *enhance productivity*

### 4. automate routine tasks
- Weak → better: *let computers do boring jobs* → **automate routine tasks**
- 意思：將例行／重複性工作自動化
- Frame: `software / robots / systems + automate routine tasks`
- Model: Chatbots can **automate routine tasks** such as answering FAQs.
- Common mistake: ~~automatic routine tasks~~（要用動詞 *automate*）

### 5. gain a competitive edge
- Weak → better: *get an advantage over others* → **gain a competitive edge**
- 意思：取得競爭優勢
- Frame: `companies / countries / start-ups + gain a competitive edge (by / through…)`
- Model: Investing in green tech can help firms **gain a competitive edge**.
- Common mistake: ~~gain competitive edge~~（要有 *a*）；~~get a competitive edge~~ 口語可以，論文多用 *gain*

## Reading passage


Hong Kong public agencies and local firms increasingly **harness the power of** artificial intelligence to triage enquiries and spot anomalies in large datasets. Paper-based workflows that once defined counter services can quickly **become obsolete** when residents expect mobile updates. Used well, automation can **enhance productivity** in back-office teams as staff **automate routine tasks** such as form checking and appointment reminders. Early movers in fintech and smart-city pilots may **gain a competitive edge** in the wider Greater Bay Area market, provided skills training keeps pace. On balance, productivity gains still depend on change management and clear rules on data use—not on tools alone.

**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊啲 routine tasks 可以用自動化處理？
2. 用自己一句解釋點解 paper-based workflows 可能 become obsolete（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話只要買工具就一定 enhance productivity，唔使管 change management。

## ANSWER KEY

### Mini drill
- A: Companies should **harness the power of** AI / technology.
- B: **obsolete**
- C: **automate** / **routine tasks**

### Passage tasks
1. **form checking** and **appointment reminders**
2. （例）residents now expect mobile updates, so counter paper processes lag behind
3. **False**（gains still depend on change management and data-use rules）
