## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. foster social cohesion
- Weak → better: *make people get along / bring people together* → **foster social cohesion**
- 意思：促進社會凝聚力（令唔同背景嘅人更有歸屬感）
- Frame: `foster social cohesion (through / among / in + community)`
- Model: District Care Teams help **foster social cohesion** by linking elderly residents with neighbours and local services.
- Common mistake: ~~foster the social cohesive~~（要用 *social cohesion*；通常唔加 *the*）

### 2. uphold the rule of law
- Weak → better: *follow the law carefully / respect the law* → **uphold the rule of law**
- 意思：維護法治（人人平等、依法辦事）
- Frame: `uphold the rule of law; be committed to upholding the rule of law`
- Model: Clear enforcement and fair procedures are needed to **uphold the rule of law** in everyday disputes.
- Common mistake: ~~uphold the rule of the law~~（固定係 *the rule of law*，*law* 前面唔再加 *the*）

### 3. enhance social mobility
- Weak → better: *help poor people move up / give people a better chance* → **enhance social mobility**
- 意思：提升社會流動性（畀基層有機會向上流動）
- Frame: `enhance / improve / promote social mobility`
- Model: Skills training and targeted scholarships can **enhance social mobility** for young people from less affluent families.
- Common mistake: ~~enhance the social mobile~~（要用 *social mobility*）

### 4. restore public confidence
- Weak → better: *make people trust again / win back trust* → **restore public confidence**
- 意思：重建公眾信心
- Frame: `restore public confidence (in + institution / service)`
- Model: Timely updates on waiting lists help **restore public confidence** in housing delivery.
- Common mistake: ~~restore the public's confidences~~（*confidence* 通常唔加複數；*public confidence* 係固定搭配）

### 5. conduct public consultation
- Weak → better: *ask the public for opinions / collect views* → **conduct public consultation**
- 意思：進行公眾諮詢（收集市民同持份者意見）
- Frame: `conduct public consultation (on + policy / proposal); after conducting public consultation`
- Model: Before expanding district services, bureaux typically **conduct public consultation** to map service gaps.
- Common mistake: ~~conduct a public consult~~（要用 *public consultation*）

## Mini drill

**A.** 填空（今日 5 個搭配）：Youth hostels and vocational pathways are designed to ________ ________ ________.
**B.** 選較好一句：(a) The bureau asked people opinions. / (b) The bureau conducted public consultation on the proposal.
**C.** 改錯：Fair courts uphold the rule of the law.

## Reading passage

Hong Kong's district-based Care Teams were expanded to bring support closer to households that face a clear **service gap**. When frontline workers connect isolated elderly residents with meals, home visits, and nearby clinics, they also help **foster social cohesion** between neighbours who rarely meet. At the same time, skills programmes and means-tested study support try to **enhance social mobility** so that background does not lock young people out of better jobs. Because trust is fragile after long waits for public housing and other services, bureaux now publish phased roll-out timelines to **restore public confidence**. Before new district measures go city-wide, officials usually **conduct public consultation** so stakeholder concerns shape the final design. Taken together, these steps show how calm, rules-based delivery can **uphold the rule of law** while still answering everyday social needs.

**Passage tasks**
1. List the five taught collocations used in the passage.
2. In one sentence, what problem does publishing roll-out timelines try to fix?
3. Match: Care Teams → ? ; skills / study support → ? ; consultation → ?（選：social mobility / cohesion / policy design）
