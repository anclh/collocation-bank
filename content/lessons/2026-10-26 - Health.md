# 2026-10-26 — Health (Lesson 22)

## Quick review（Lesson 21）

1. **harness the power of** — 善用／駕馭……嘅力量
2. **become obsolete** — 變得過時／被淘汰
3. **enhance productivity** — 提高生產力／工作效率

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. maintain a balanced diet
- Weak → better: *eat healthy food / have a good diet* → **maintain a balanced diet**
- 意思：保持均衡飲食
- Frame: `maintain a balanced diet (+ of fruit, vegetables, and whole grains)`
- Model: Teenagers who **maintain a balanced diet** tend to concentrate better at school.
- Common mistake: ~~maintain balanced diet~~（要有 *a*）；論文多用 *maintain* 多過 *keep*

### 2. be prone to
- Weak → better: *easily get / often get* → **be prone to**
- 意思：容易（患上／出現……）
- Frame: `be prone to + illness / injury / stress / infection`
- Model: Office workers who sit all day are **prone to** back pain.
- Common mistake: ~~prone for~~ / ~~prone of~~（介詞要用 *to*）

### 3. seek medical attention
- Weak → better: *go to the doctor / see a doctor* → **seek medical attention**
- 意思：求醫／尋求醫療協助
- Frame: `seek medical attention (+ immediately / for + symptom)`
- Model: Anyone with a high fever should **seek medical attention** without delay.
- Common mistake: ~~seek for medical attention~~（*seek* 後面唔使 *for*）

### 4. curb the spread of
- Weak → better: *stop / reduce the spreading of* → **curb the spread of**
- 意思：遏止……嘅傳播（疾病、病毒）
- Frame: `curb the spread of + disease / virus / infection`
- Model: Masks and vaccines help **curb the spread of** infectious diseases.
- Common mistake: ~~curb spreading of~~（要有 *the spread of*）

### 5. promote mental well-being
- Weak → better: *make people feel happier / look after mental health* → **promote mental well-being**
- 意思：促進心理健康／精神健康
- Frame: `promote mental well-being (+ through / by + V-ing)`
- Model: Schools can **promote mental well-being** through counselling and peer support.
- Common mistake: ~~promote the mental well-being~~（通常唔加 *the*）；*well-being* 有連字號較常見

## Reading passage

Many city workers skip breakfast and rely on takeaways, so they struggle to **maintain a balanced diet** across a busy week. Over time, irregular meals and long hours at a desk leave them **prone to** fatigue and minor infections. When symptoms linger, doctors advise staff to **seek medical attention** early rather than wait until they can barely work. During flu season, employers also try to **curb the spread of** viruses by encouraging sick leave and better ventilation. Beyond physical care, progressive companies **promote mental well-being** through flexible hours and counselling, recognising that stress and poor sleep feed into the same cycle of ill health. Together, these steps turn a reactive sick-leave culture into a more preventive one.

## ANSWER KEY

### Mini drill
- A: People should **maintain a balanced diet** every day.（或 Employees ought to maintain a balanced diet.）
- B: **prone to**
- C: **curb the spread of**

### Passage tasks
1. **sick leave** and **better ventilation**（masks 文中冇直接寫，唔好亂加）
2.（例）early treatment stops symptoms getting worse / 早啲睇醫生可以避免惡化
3. **False**（文尾講 stress 同 ill health 係同一個循環）
