# 2026-10-05 - Solutions and Measures (Lesson 16)

## Today's 5 collocations

1. **take decisive action** (weak: do something)
   - 果斷採取行動，唔再拖。
   - Frame: [Govt/Sb] must take decisive action to + V / against + N
   - Model: Governments must take decisive action to curb traffic congestion.
   - Mistake: ~~make decisive action~~ → take action

2. **find a viable solution to** (weak: a good way)
   - 搵到一個實際可行嘅解決方法（viable = 行得通）。
   - Frame: find a viable solution to + N
   - Model: Experts are still trying to find a viable solution to the housing shortage.
   - Mistake: ~~a solution of the problem~~ → a solution to the problem

3. **alleviate the pressure on** (weak: make it less bad)
   - 紓緩、減輕壓力或問題，但唔係完全解決。
   - Frame: [Measure] can help (to) alleviate the pressure on + N
   - Model: Expanding the railway network would alleviate the pressure on roads.
   - Mistake: using alleviate to mean "solve completely" → use solve / resolve

4. **raise public awareness of** (weak: tell people more about)
   - 提高公眾對某事嘅意識。
   - Frame: raise public awareness of + N / of the need to + V
   - Model: Campaigns can raise public awareness of the benefits of walking.
   - Mistake: ~~rise public awareness~~ → raise (needs an object)

5. **provide incentives for sb to + V** (weak: encourage)
   - 提供誘因或獎勵，令人願意做某事。
   - Frame: provide incentives for + sb + to V
   - Model: The government could provide incentives for drivers to switch to public transport.
   - Mistake: ~~provide incentives for drivers switching~~ → for drivers to switch

## Reading passage

Prompt: Traffic congestion is a growing problem in many cities. What measures can be taken to tackle it?

Many major cities are *plagued by* traffic congestion, which wastes time and pollutes the air. To **find a viable solution to** this problem, governments need to **take decisive action** rather than simply building more roads. One effective measure is to invest in fast and affordable public transport, which would **alleviate the pressure on** busy streets. In addition, authorities could **provide incentives for** commuters **to** travel outside peak hours, such as cheaper fares in the early morning. Finally, campaigns can **raise public awareness of** the hidden costs of driving, including its impact on health and the environment. Although no single policy can eliminate congestion entirely, a combination of these steps would make urban life considerably more pleasant.


**Passage tasks**
1. 文中邊啲做法可以 alleviate the pressure on busy streets？
2. 用自己說話解釋點解要 take decisive action 而唔係淨係起多啲路（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話單一政策就可以完全消除塞車。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **fast/affordable public transport**（亦有 off-peak incentives）
2.（例）building more roads alone is not enough / need broader measures
3. **False**（no single policy can eliminate congestion）
