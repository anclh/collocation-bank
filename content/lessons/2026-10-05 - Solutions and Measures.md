# 2026-10-05 - Solutions and Measures (Lesson 16)

## Today's 5 collocations

1. **take decisive action** (weak: do something)
   - 果斷採取行動，唔再拖。
   - Frame: [Govt/Sb] must take decisive action to + V / against + N
   - Model: Governments must take decisive action to curb traffic congestion.
   - Mistake: ~~make decisive action~~ → take action

2. **find a viable solution to** (weak: a good way)
   - 搵到一個實際可行嘅解決方法（viable = 行得通）。
   - Frame: find a viable solution to + N
   - Model: Experts are still trying to find a viable solution to the housing shortage.
   - Mistake: ~~a solution of the problem~~ → a solution to the problem

3. **alleviate the pressure on** (weak: make it less bad)
   - 紓緩、減輕壓力或問題，但唔係完全解決。
   - Frame: [Measure] can help (to) alleviate the pressure on + N
   - Model: Expanding the railway network would alleviate the pressure on roads.
   - Mistake: using alleviate to mean "solve completely" → use solve / resolve

4. **raise public awareness of** (weak: tell people more about)
   - 提高公眾對某事嘅意識。
   - Frame: raise public awareness of + N / of the need to + V
   - Model: Campaigns can raise public awareness of the benefits of walking.
   - Mistake: ~~rise public awareness~~ → raise (needs an object)

5. **provide incentives for sb to + V** (weak: encourage)
   - 提供誘因或獎勵，令人願意做某事。
   - Frame: provide incentives for + sb + to V
   - Model: The government could provide incentives for drivers to switch to public transport.
   - Mistake: ~~provide incentives for drivers switching~~ → for drivers to switch

## Reading passage


Congested corridors into the urban core force transport planners to **take decisive action** rather than rely on short-term traffic police deployments alone. Expanding railway capacity is often framed as a way to **find a viable solution to** peak-hour gridlock, while park-and-ride and feeder buses **alleviate the pressure on** limited road space. Public campaigns **raise public awareness of** walking and cycling for short trips, especially near new Northern Metropolis employment nodes. The Government may also **provide incentives for** drivers **to** switch to public transport or cleaner vehicles, subject to the fiscal envelope and charging infrastructure. On balance, a mix of supply-side upgrades and demand management tends to outperform any single slogan measure.
