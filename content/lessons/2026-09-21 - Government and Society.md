# Lesson 11 — Government and Society（政府與社會）

Date: 2026-09-21

## Today's 5 collocations

### 1. introduce legislation
- Weak: make new laws
- 意思：推出法例
- Frame: `introduce legislation to + V / on + N`
- Model: The government plans to introduce legislation to curb online hate speech.
- Mistake: legislation 係不可數，唔寫 a legislation

### 2. enforce the law
- Weak: make people follow the law
- 意思：執法
- Frame: `enforce the law / enforce regulations`
- Model: Passing a bill is not enough; authorities must also enforce the law.
- Mistake: enforce 唔等於 reinforce

### 3. allocate public funds
- Weak: give money to
- 意思：撥出公帑
- Frame: `allocate public funds to + N / for + V-ing`
- Model: The council decided to allocate public funds to affordable housing.
- Mistake: 較好用 allocate to/for

### 4. maintain social stability
- Weak: keep society calm
- 意思：維持社會穩定
- Frame: `maintain social stability`
- Model: Fair policing helps maintain social stability in diverse cities.
- Mistake: the social stable → 用 social stability

### 5. tackle social inequality
- Weak: fix the rich-poor problem
- 意思：應對社會不平等
- Frame: `tackle social inequality`
- Model: Progressive taxation is one way to tackle social inequality.
- Mistake: solve completely 太絕對；用 tackle/address/reduce

## Reading passage


To curb online investment scams targeting retail investors in Hong Kong, authorities plan to **introduce legislation** that tightens platform duties and disclosure rules. Agencies must still **enforce the law** consistently so that new offences deter repeat operators rather than displace them overseas. The Government continues to **allocate public funds** to public education and reporting channels, recognising that prosecution alone cannot rebuild trust. Clearer consumer protection can help **maintain social stability** when losses cluster in middle-income districts. Over time, better redress and means-tested support may also **tackle social inequality** between victims who can afford legal advice and those who cannot.

**Passage tasks**
1. 新法例主要針對邊種騙案？
2. 用自己一句解釋點解 allocate public funds to education 仍然重要（1 句）
3. True / False：Passage 話單靠 prosecution 已經足夠 rebuild trust。

## ANSWER KEY

### Passage tasks
1. **online investment scams** targeting retail investors
2. （例）prosecution alone cannot rebuild trust; prevention and reporting channels matter
3. **False**（prosecution alone cannot rebuild trust）
